# Shopify-Inventory-Optimization-Report
This Report Focuses on Analyzing Inventory Health, sales performance, warehouse efficiency, returns, and gross profit trends. The dashboard enabled Quick monitoring of stock status, demand patterns, and channel-wise performance.


**Problem Statement**

Businesses faced challenges in maintaining optimal inventory levels across warehouses and sales channels, leading to stock shortages, overstocking, delayed replenishment, and reduced profitability. The goal was to build a data-driven inventory optimization dashboard for better stock visibility and operational efficiency.

**Tools and Tech Used**

Power BI ,Power Query ,DAX Calculations,Excel


**Key Challenges Faced**

Managing large inventory datasets with multiple warehouse and channel mappings was a major challenge. Building accurate stock health logic, demand categorization, and performance-optimized DAX calculations required careful data modeling and transformation.


**Project Impact on Business**


* The dashboard provided end-to-end visibility into inventory performance, helping businesses track stock levels, warehouse utilization, sales trends, and profitability in quick time. This improved operational transparency and enabled faster, data-driven decision-making across inventory and supply chain operations.

* By identifying critical stock products, reorder risks, and low-demand inventory, the report helped optimize inventory planning and reduce excess holding costs. Businesses could maintain better stock availability for high-demand products while minimizing overstocking and inventory wastage.

* The analysis of warehouse-wise and channel-wise performance enabled better resource allocation and fulfillment planning. Insights into returns, damaged units, and gross profit trends also supported profitability improvement and operational efficiency enhancement.

* Improved inventory visibility across warehouses and sales channels, helping reduce stockout risks and excess inventory costs. Enabled faster business decisions through KPI-driven insights on revenue, gross profit, returns, and stock health.

* Overall, the dashboard helped improve inventory control, reduce stock-related business risks, optimize replenishment strategies, and enhance revenue and margin performance across multiple sales channels.


**Key Learnings**

Gained hands-on experience in inventory analytics, stock optimization strategies, and supply chain KPI reporting using Power BI. Improved expertise in DAX, data modeling, dashboard storytelling, and business insight generation.

**Top Recommendations to Business**

* Maintain safety stock levels for high-demand products to avoid stockouts

* Optimize reorder points using demand and sales trends

* Reduce excess inventory in low-demand products to minimize holding costs

* Focus on high-margin products and top-performing sales channels

* Monitor product returns and damaged units to improve operational efficiency

* Implement predictive inventory forecasting for smarter replenishment planning
